Local Business OS

A content publishing tool for local businesses.

Local Business OS helps local businesses plan, review and publish organic posts to their own Facebook Pages, and shows them how those posts perform.

  • Every business connects only its own Page, through Meta's official login.
  • Nothing is published without the business's approval.
  • The tool does not run ads, does not sell data and does not access personal messages.
