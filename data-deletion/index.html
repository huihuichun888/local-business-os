Data Deletion Instructions

Local Business OS · Effective 2026-09-22

You can have all data the Service holds about your Facebook Page deleted at any time. Use either option below.

Option 1: Remove the Service in Facebook

  1. In Facebook, open Settings & privacy → Settings → Business Integrations.
  2. Find Local Business OS Publisher and click Remove.

This stops the Service from accessing your Page right away. The Service's stored access token stops working, and the next automatic connection check marks your connection as disconnected. To also delete the records we still hold, use Option 2.

Option 2: Email us a deletion request

Send an email to guo091109@hotmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request". Include:

  • The name of your Facebook Page, and its Page ID if you know it.
  • The name of your business.

After we confirm the request comes from someone authorized for that Page, we:

  • delete the stored Page access token right away;
  • within 30 days, delete the Page connection records, the post identifiers and the performance data we hold for that Page;
  • reply to confirm the deletion.

We never store Facebook passwords or personal user access tokens, so there is nothing of that kind to delete.

Questions: guo091109@hotmail.com
