Privacy Policy

Local Business OS · Effective 2026-09-22

This policy explains what information Local Business OS ("the Service", "we") collects when a business connects its Facebook Page, how that information is used, and how it can be deleted. The Service is operated by Local Business OS.

1. Who uses the Service

The Service is used by local businesses, and by people those businesses authorize to manage their social media, to publish organic posts to the business's own Facebook Page. It is not intended for consumers or for anyone under 18.

2. Information we receive from Meta

When a person connects a Facebook Page using Facebook Login for Business, they choose which Page to share. For that Page only, we receive:

  • Page identifiers: the Page ID and Page name.
  • A Page access token, which lets the Service publish approved posts and read performance data for that Page.
  • Post identifiers for posts the Service publishes.
  • Post-level performance metrics provided by Meta, such as views, unique viewers, clicks, reactions, comments and shares.
  • Basic profile information (name and profile picture) that Facebook Login provides about the person authorizing the connection. We use it only to show who connected the Page, and we do not store the personal user access token.

We request only these permissions: pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, pages_manage_posts and read_insights. We do not request access to Business Portfolios, ad accounts, Instagram accounts, Messenger conversations, or other Pages the person did not select.

3. Information the business provides

Businesses provide their own business details, photos and approved post content so that posts can be prepared and published. This content belongs to the business.

4. How we use information

  • To publish posts that the business has reviewed and approved, to the business's own Page.
  • To collect post performance at set intervals (for example 24 hours, 72 hours and 7 days after publishing) and report it back to the same business.
  • To check that the connection still works and to tell the business when it needs to be reconnected.

We do not sell information. We do not use Meta Platform Data for advertising, profiling, or building audiences, and we do not share one business's data with another business.

5. Sharing

We share information only with service providers that host the Service's database and infrastructure, under confidentiality and security obligations, and only as needed to run the Service. We may also disclose information if required by law.

6. Storage and security

  • Page access tokens are stored encrypted in a secrets vault and are never shown in the product interface.
  • Each business's connection and data are kept separate by business, and one Facebook Page can be linked to only one business.
  • Access to the system is restricted to authorized operators.
  • We never ask for, receive or store Facebook passwords.

7. Retention

We keep a Page's connection and performance data while the business uses the Service. When a business disconnects, or asks us to delete its data, we delete its stored access token right away and delete its connection records and performance data within 30 days, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

8. Your choices and rights

  • You can remove the Service's access at any time in Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Business Integrations. Select "Local Business OS Publisher" and click Remove.
  • You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data. See the Data Deletion Instructions.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above. If a change is material, we will also notify connected businesses.
